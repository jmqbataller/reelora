# Preservation Rules

Reelora is a real-footage editor first. Product and model fidelity must never be sacrificed for a more dramatic edit.

## Model preservation

Do not alter, replace, regenerate, beautify, reshape, or restyle the model. Preserve the original face, identity, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, hands, pose, and movement.

## Product preservation

Do not alter the product's design, fabric, texture, weave, ribbing, color, print, logo, tags, size markings, stitching, neckline, sleeves, straps, pockets, fit, length, dimensions, folds, or construction.

## Safe operations

Allowed operations include trimming, cutting, rearranging, cropping, reframing, stabilization, keyframed pan/zoom, speed changes, transitions, fades, audio leveling, and conservative exposure/white-balance corrections.

## Unsafe operations

Do not use generative fill, outpainting, face replacement, body reshaping, garment reconstruction, fabric synthesis, logo reconstruction, or AI-generated accessories/backgrounds.

## Conflict rule

If an enhancement risks changing the model or product, simplify or remove the enhancement. **Preservation always wins.**
